Captain's Log · v1.84.0 · 9 November 2026

Readable bounty posters, a hunt tally at sea

Ahoy, Captain!

v1.84.0 (build 164) is on its way to Google Play. It is a small update with a few things that make bounty hunting and sailing near the forts nicer.

The wanted posters on the Bounty Board are now a good read. Tap any poster and it opens at double size with the outlaw's name, the task and the full story behind the bounty, the part the board never had room for. Accept or Close it right there.

Out at sea you now always know how your hunt is going. While a hunt is on, the poster sits under your minimap with a tally beneath it, like 12 of 22, and the number moves the moment a hull goes under. No more sailing home to the board to check.

The forts around the islands now stop your ship at their real waterline instead of an invisible box around them, so you can sail close and slip behind them through the open water. The combat level bar also moved down a little so it no longer hides under the Base and Ship buttons.

Thanks for sailing with us. If you spot a problem, tap your ship and choose Report bug.

Captain's Log · v1.83.0 · 6 November 2026

Pirate squads, deadly storms, a new sea bed

Ahoy, Captain!

v1.83.0 (build 163) is on its way to Google Play, and the sea just got a lot more dangerous. Pirates, storms, new waters and bigger rewards are all waiting for you.

Pirate squads now roam the whole map. 25 squads of orange-sailed pirates, 62 ships in all, are always under way, from lone sloops near home to seven frigates in the far water. Sink them for hero XP. Provoke one ship and her whole squad hunts you, and a beaten squad comes back 30 minutes later.

There is a new bounty on the board: the Pirate Ship Hunt. Sink 15 to 25 pirates anywhere at sea within 6 hours for silver and a bonus, sometimes even an expert puzzle piece. There is always one on the board. Bounties also pay more now, and new bounties and courier contracts follow each other with no refill wait. You can move the Bounty Board around your island too.

Storms bite back. Sailing through one now costs more hull, your helm turns heavy, and lightning takes aim: watch for the ring on the water and get out before the bolt lands.

The sea has a new look. The shallows now show a real sea bed with sand and sea grass, and the waves have been retuned. Islands also collide by their true shape, so palm trees no longer trap your ship on the shore.

Bigger prizes: the Black Death Kraken now pays 250,000 Silver on top of its Gold, and Castle Scullywagger adds 70,000 Silver to its reward and mends 10 HP a second out of range. Careful with the factions though: sinking their ships now costs twice the reputation.

In the Tavern, Shark Hunt is faster, Checkers lets you choose any capture and has smarter opponents, and Beat the Monkey is one round with draws replayed. In the shop, an expert puzzle piece now costs 70 gems.

We also did a big performance pass across the whole game, so everything should run smoother. The intro video is now off by default, and you can turn it on in Options.

Thanks for sailing with us. If you spot a problem, tap your ship and choose Report bug.

Captain's Log · v1.81.0 · 27 October 2026

Move your buildings, a bigger chart

Ahoy, Captain!

v1.81.0 (build 160) is on its way to Google Play, with a bigger chart for navigating, a new deal at Castle Scullywagger and a handful of fixes.

The World Map is bigger when you navigate. In navigation mode the chart now shows the sea alone across the full width of the screen, without the title, legend and paper edges. The corner minimap steps aside while the chart is open.

Castle Scullywagger has changed. Taking the castle now pays 2 Gold and a Blueprint. The castle also mends much more slowly when you sail out of range, only 2 HP a second instead of 50, so you can pull back, repair and come back for another pass without starting over.

Did you know you can arrange your island your way? Press and hold any building until it lifts, drag it to a free spot and tap to set it down. A green frame means it fits. Your Port and Castillo stay where they are.

Fixes: an island card can no longer leave its Close button off the bottom of the screen when you open it again. The sea now pauses while an island card is open, so nobody can sink you while you read it. In the Swine Hunt, Exit now asks before it ends your run.

Thanks for sailing with us. If you spot a problem, tap your ship and choose Report bug.

Captain's Log · v1.80.0 · 25 October 2026

Courier Contracts

Ahoy, Captain!

v1.80.0 (build 159) is on its way to Google Play, with Courier Contracts, a bigger Bounty Board and faster Gunnery training.

Courier Contracts are here. The Bounty Board now has a Courier column next to the wanted posters. Pick a contract, sail to the pickup island in Ocean Tycoon and load the sealed crate into your hold, then bring it to the drop-off before time runs out. Claim your silver at the Bounty Board, and your hero earns merchant XP too. Each delivery also raises your reputation with the faction that sent it. There are 100 contracts across 46 islands. Longer routes and richer cargo pay more.

Tempted by what's inside? Back at Base Island you can break the seal and keep the cargo. But the sender won't forget it. You lose reputation with that faction, gain a wanted skull, and nobody will trust you with cargo for 8 hours.

The wanted side grows too. The board now shows three wanted posters at a time, so you choose which outlaw to hunt, and a new one is posted 15 minutes after you claim. If your warehouse is too full for the whole reward, the board tells you exactly how much it took.

Captain Gunnery now trains much faster: about 1,000 XP for every hour at sea, ten times more than before.

Thanks for sailing with us. If you spot a problem, tap your ship and choose Report bug.
