The game

A pirate's life, one cove at a time

Curse of Scully mixes base building, fleet command, open-sea sailing and online multiplayer. Grow your harbour while you play, then take the helm yourself.

Build

Build your pirate haven

Start with a quiet cove and grow it into a busy harbour town. Your buildings gather wood, ore and water while you play, and you can pick up any building or lantern post and move it where you like.

  • Over 20 buildings to raise and upgrade
  • A Water Well that climbs all the way to level 100
  • Night lanterns that light the docks after dark
Sail

Sail a living ocean

In Ocean Tycoon you steer your own ship between the islands. Storms hit hard and your hull creaks as the weather turns. Brave the rain to Castle Scullywagger, grab supply drops, and follow a new treasure chart every day.

  • Islands from Treasure Cove to arctic snow
  • Real storms, snow and night crossings
  • Play with touch, or set your own keyboard keys
Online multiplayer

Grand Horizon

Take your fleet online. Grand Horizon is a shared sea full of real captains and pirate lords. Scout your rivals, raid their warehouses, guard your own harbour and build an alliance that rules the map.

Your island Rival captain Alliance harbour Pirate lord
  • Attack fleet
  • Trade convoy
  • Scout

Raid rival captains

Scout an island to see what it guards, then send your fleet to break its Castillo and sail home with the loot. Keep your own defences strong, because rivals will come for yours.

Alliances

Found or join a Den of Pirates of up to 15 captains. Share a treasury, rise through the ranks and park your ships in allies' harbours to help defend them.

War Islands

Garrison War Islands with your fleet and collect tribute for as long as you can hold them.

Trade and intercept

Sell goods to other captains on the market and send convoys to collect them. A convoy at sea is a target, so plan your route.

Hire raiders

Short on ships? Hire raiders to strike an island for you.

Pirate lords and events

The Kraken King and Storm Empress Nyx rule the far waters. Horizon events bring the Nyx Kraken hunt and whaling grounds you fish with other captains.
